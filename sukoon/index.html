Quick troubleshooting

  • Prayer times: Confirm city, time zone, calculation method and manual adjustments. Follow your trusted local timetable when differences occur.
  • Qibla: Allow location or select a city. Keep the device away from magnetic interference; compass guidance depends on sensor accuracy.
  • Notifications and Athan: Check iOS notification permission, Sukoon reminder settings, sound options and Focus mode.
  • Privacy and personal data: Open Settings → Privacy & Data to review export and deletion controls. See the full privacy policy.
